Outreach
A Heart for the Lost
 
“For the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost.”
Luke 19:10

Christ came to seek and to save the lost, and He calls His people to share that same heart. The need for truth in the streets of San Diego is ever-present, and the harvest ever-plentiful. While our missions teams carry the gospel to the nations, the mission of the Great Commission continues right here at home.

How will lost souls learn of the gospel if there is no one to preach it to them? Through our Reach Ministry, we go out together to our city, our neighborhoods, and our college campuses so that the gospel is preached, people are loved, and souls are won for Christ.

Open-Air Evangelism
Project Proclaim

Project Proclaim takes the gospel to the streets of downtown San Diego through open-air preaching, one-on-one evangelism, and handing out Gospel tracts, followed by food and fellowship. Our most recent Project Proclaim brought the hope of Christ to the thousands gathered at San Diego Comic-Con, complete with limited edition Gospel Comic Tracts!

Neighborhood
Winning Our Neighbors

Winning Our Neighbors sends teams out into the neighborhoods of San Diego to share the gospel door to door. Just as the Lord has borne fruit on the college campus, we are eager to see how He will work in our own neighborhood, so that the gospel is preached, our neighbors are loved, and their souls are won for Christ.

Short-Term Missions
Homefield Missions

Homefield Missions is our evangelism-focused short-term missions trip right here in San Diego. For a whole week during SDSU's Welcome Week, our team lives in together, shares meals and fellowship, and boldly reaches the campus through flyering, Welcome Week events, and evangelism. This year's theme is “Mighty in Spirit” (Aug 23–29, 2026).
